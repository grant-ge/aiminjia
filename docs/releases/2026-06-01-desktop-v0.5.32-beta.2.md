## Product Changes

- Personal auth flows now surface server-side error details in the login, registration, and reset-password paths, including wrong-password failures.
- Auth-scoped runtime state is isolated more carefully when users sign out and switch accounts on the same machine, reducing stale resources from the previous account.
- Expert team i18n coverage was expanded across the entry page, team templates, chat source label, director prompts, process panel, detail drawer, and expand/collapse labels.
- Expert team director prompt generation now uses stable internal team identifiers while keeping user-visible team names localized.
