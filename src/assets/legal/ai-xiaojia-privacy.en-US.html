2. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

We may collect and use your personal information in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, necessity, and good faith. If you refuse to provide certain necessary information, you may be unable to use the related features, but other basic functions are generally not affected.

(1) Login and Identity Verification

When you register and log in to AIjia, we may collect your DingTalk account identifier, nickname, avatar, enterprise organization identity, and device login information. These are used to verify user information, adapt to different operating systems, and create your account so that you can log in and use the full product or service.

To safeguard the account, we may collect login time, login device, IP address, login location, account status, operation log, and abnormal-risk information, and use them for account protection, security monitoring, and risk identification.

(2) Providing Intelligent Dialogue and AI-Assisted Operations

Intelligent dialogue and AI-assisted operations are designed to enable AI models to understand your Input Content and to return responses or perform tasks. When you use the Service with our default model, the Input Content that you actively send through the chat box or authorize us to access — including text, voice, files, video, images, and instructions (collectively, "Input Content") — is collected and processed by us to provide the Service. When you configure and use a third-party large model that you obtained on your own ("Custom Model"), your Input Content is sent directly by you to the third-party large model, which processes it independently and returns Output Content directly to you.

If the Input Content, feedback, or other information you provide may contain personal information of others, please obtain lawful authorization from them before providing it, to avoid inappropriate disclosure of others' personal information.

(3) Plugins, Skills, Extensions, and Custom Model Integration

You may use or integrate plugins, skills, extensions, custom models, third-party APIs, third-party MCP services, or other third-party services. To enable the corresponding functions, we may collect and transmit necessary information to the relevant service provider, including your task request, context, parameters, invocation records, device environment information, account identifier, and execution results.

If you integrate a Custom Model that you configured yourself, your Input Content, context, and Output Content may be processed independently by that third-party model provider. In such cases, we may provide only API connectivity, task orchestration, UI display, or log management.

You should carefully select and integrate Third-Party Services and bear the resulting fees, risks, compliance obligations, and liabilities.

(4) Payment, Subscription, and Transaction Services

When you subscribe to membership, purchase Tokens, buy value-added services, or otherwise transact, we may collect your order information, transaction amount, payment status, payment time, payment channel, refund information, and after-sales records.

The actual payment process is typically handled by a third-party payment institution. We do not directly obtain your full payment account password, bank card password, or payment passcode, but may receive payment results, transaction serial numbers, and other necessary information for transaction completion, financial reconciliation, customer support, dispute handling, and risk control.

(5) Customer Service, Complaints, Feedback, and Dispute Handling

When you consult, complain, report, request after-sales support, provide feedback, or assert rights, we may collect your name, contact information, account information, problem description, related screenshots, audio/video, email content, communication/call records, order information, log information, evidentiary materials, and other information we deem necessary to verify identity and handle the dispute.

To maintain the normal operation of AIjia, identify abnormal accounts, prevent security risks, troubleshoot, optimize performance, and analyze statistics, we may collect:

  • Device information: device model, brand, operating system, system version, CPU architecture, memory, disk, application version, resolution, language, timezone, network status, device identifier;

This choice will not adversely affect your use of basic product functions.
